# Singology Alpha: Founder's Guide

## Getting in

1. Open **https://singology-facade.shipborn.workers.dev**
2. Cloudflare asks for your email. Enter `mark@de-lisser.com`, click **Send login code**, and type in the code it emails you.
3. On the Singology sign-in page, enter the same email. Open the email from **alpha@shipbornsoftwaresolutions.co.uk** and click its link.
4. You land on your **Review queue**.

## What you can try now

Everything you do here is saved for real.

- **Review queue:** students' takes waiting for your feedback.
- **Reviewing a take:** play, skip and loop the recording; pin comments to exact moments; write a summary; assign exercises; send. The student gets an email saying their feedback is ready.
- **Recently reviewed:** reopen anything you've sent.
- **Exercise library:** browse the exercises you can assign.

The recordings are made-up test tones, not real singing.

## What waits for your go-ahead

**Students uploading their own audio and video recordings, and you listening to and watching them.** At the moment, an upload checks the file but doesn't save it.

This needs two paid services:

- **Bunny** (stores and streams the recordings): a 14-day free trial with no card, then a card for about $1 a month.
- **Cloudflare Workers Paid** (prepares each upload for playback): $5 a month.

That's about $6 a month during the alpha. When you give the go-ahead, I'll handle the setup, send you the one link you need, and start building straight away.

Send any problems or impressions to me.
